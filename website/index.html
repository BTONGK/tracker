Get started

Up and running in 3 steps

The Chrome extension connects to your personal dashboard. Here's exactly how to set it up.

1
Install the Chrome extension
Download the extension, open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions. Enable Developer mode, click "Load unpacked," and select the unzipped folder.
⬇ Download extension (.zip)
2
Create your dashboard & copy your key
Open the dashboard. On first visit it generates a unique workspace key — click the ⚙ settings icon and copy it.
Open dashboard →
3
Connect extension to your dashboard
Open Gmail and press ⌥T on Mac (or Alt+T on Windows). Paste your workspace key when prompted — you're connected. Every ⌥T from now on captures a task straight to your dashboard.
How it works

From inbox to graph

One keypress captures the task. The dashboard shows you everything — list, focus view, and an interactive graph of how tasks relate.

01

Open any email

Go to Gmail and open a thread that needs action — a request, approval, or follow-up.

02

Press ⌥T

AI reads the thread and fills in the task title, priority, and action type. Review and save in seconds.

03

See the graph

Tasks land in your dashboard and link to related ones automatically — by keyword, person, or project.

Privacy

Your emails stay yours.

We built Topolist with a clear rule: your inbox is private. Here's exactly what happens when you press ⌥T.

🔒

Emails are never stored

When you capture a task, the email content is sent to AI to extract the action — then immediately discarded. Nothing from your inbox is saved on our servers.

👁

We never read your email

Topolist has no background access to your Gmail. The extension only reads the email you have open at the moment you press ⌥T — nothing else, ever.

🗂

Only task data is saved

The only thing stored is the task itself — title, priority, and your notes. Your workspace is tied to a private key only you control.
