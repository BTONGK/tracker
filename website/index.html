How it works

From inbox to done
in three steps

No complicated setup. Install in 60 seconds and start capturing immediately.

Step 01
🔌

Install the extension

Add Topolist to Chrome in one click. Sign up for a free account — no credit card needed.

Step 02
⚡

Capture with Alt+T

Open any email in Gmail and press Alt+T. AI reads the thread and creates a task with the right title, priority, and assignees — in seconds.

Step 03
✦

Track everything

All your tasks live in one dashboard. See them as a list, focus view, or interactive graph that shows how tasks connect.

Features

Built for people who
live in their inbox

Everything you need to stop losing track of things that matter.

🧠

AI task extraction

Claude reads your entire email thread and pulls out the exact action needed — not just the subject line. Who needs to reply, by when, to whom.

🕸

Task graph

See how your tasks connect. Related emails, same project, same person — Topolist surfaces the links automatically so nothing falls through.

⚡

Keyboard-first capture

One shortcut from anywhere. Alt+T opens the capture overlay without leaving the page, with AI filling in the details instantly.

🎯

Smart prioritization

Tasks are auto-tagged with priority, action type (reply, review, approve), and due dates pulled from the email content.

👥

Assignee tracking

Know who's cc'd, who sent it, and who needs to act. Hand off tasks with a note — and track whether they were done.

📎

Attachments & notes

Attach files, add context notes, and comment on tasks. Everything stays together so you never have to dig through email again.
