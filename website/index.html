Get started

Up and running
in 3 steps

The Chrome extension connects to your personal dashboard. Here's exactly how to set it up.

1
Install the Chrome extension
Download the extension below, then open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked," and select the unzipped folder.
⬇ Download extension (.zip)
2
Create your dashboard & copy your key
Open the Topolist dashboard. On your first visit it generates a unique workspace key — click the ⚙ settings icon in the top-right and copy it.
Open dashboard →
3
Connect extension to your dashboard
Open Gmail and press Alt+T. The extension will ask for your workspace key — paste it in and click Save. You're connected! From now on, every Alt+T captures a task straight to your dashboard.

Your workspace key

The key links the extension to your private dashboard. Think of it like a password — copy it from the dashboard settings and paste it into the extension prompt.

How it works

From inbox to done
in seconds

Once the extension is connected, capturing tasks is one keypress away — no copy-paste, no tab-switching.

Step 01
📧

Open any email

Go to Gmail and open an email thread. Could be a request, a follow-up, an approval — anything that needs action.

Step 02
⚡

Press Alt+T

A capture overlay appears right in Gmail. AI reads the thread and fills in the task title, priority, sender, and type — you just review and save.

Step 03
✦

Track in your dashboard

The task lands in your Topolist dashboard instantly. See everything as a list, focus view, or interactive graph showing how tasks connect.

Features

Built for people who
live in their inbox

Everything you need to stop losing track of things that matter.

🧠

AI task extraction

Claude reads your entire email thread and pulls out the exact action needed — who needs to reply, by when, to whom — not just the subject line.

🕸

Task graph

See how your tasks connect. Related emails, same project, same person — Topolist surfaces the links so nothing falls through the cracks.

⚡

Keyboard-first capture

One shortcut from anywhere. Alt+T opens the capture overlay without leaving Gmail, with AI filling in the details instantly.

🎯

Smart prioritization

Tasks are auto-tagged with priority, action type (reply, review, approve), and due dates pulled from the email content.

🔑

No account needed

Your workspace key is your identity. No email signup, no passwords — just generate a key and start capturing. Your data stays yours.

📎

Attachments & notes

Attach files, add context notes, and comment on tasks. Everything stays together so you never dig through email again.
